Six Specialized Autonomous Agents

Each stage of procurement handled with dedicated algorithmic intelligence and strict policy governance.

📝

1. Smart Requirement Analysis

Parses structured forms and natural language requests, extracting hardware specifications, deadlines, and budget constraints into standardized schemas.

⚖️

2. Intelligent Vendor Comparison

Evaluates suppliers against mandatory criteria, eliminates non-compliant proposals, and ranks eligible suppliers using multi-criteria weighted scoring.

💰

3. Budget and Policy Validation

Enforces department fiscal quotas, determines required executive approval tiers, and checks compliance before committing capital.

📄

4. Automated Purchase Orders

Generates tamper-evident Purchase Orders upon authorized approval, prevents duplication, and synchronizes expected inventory receipts.

🔍

5. Invoice Matching & Exceptions

Extracts supplier PDF/image invoices using OCR, executes strict 3-way matching across PO and Goods Receipts, and flags discrepancies.

💳

6. Controlled Payment Processing

Enforces payment authorizations, verifies idempotency keys to stop duplicate payments, and executes simulated settlement with verifiable audit records.
